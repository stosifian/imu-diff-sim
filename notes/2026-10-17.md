### OFAT RESULT (64 episodes x 1000 steps, clean-trained policy, /runs/go1_clean.pkl)

Baseline: return 32.18, tracking MSE 0.0351, fall rate 0.00, survival 1.00.
Figures: reports/figures/ofat-white-vs-bias.png, ofat-all-factors.png

| factor | 10% knee | worst tested | vs BMI270 |
|---|---|---|---|
| gyro white noise | 0.5012 rad/s | 0.82 return, no falls | 635x |
| gyro persistent bias | none in range | 0.91 return, 2% falls | 57x, no knee |
| gyro scale error | none in range | 0.97 return at 50% error | 25x, no knee |
| gravity tilt | 0.1995 | 0.11 return, 91% falls | ~10x |
| IMU latency | 2 steps (40 ms) | total collapse by 3 | ~7x |

- [SURPRISE] THE STRUCTURAL HYPOTHESIS IS REFUTED. White noise and persistent
  bias produce nearly IDENTICAL curves at equal magnitude, and bias is if
  anything slightly better tolerated (0.91 vs 0.82 at 0.5 rad/s). The
  1/sqrt(N) argument -- that i.i.d. noise averages away while a constant
  offset integrates -- predicted bias would knee at far lower magnitude. It
  does not. That argument was the project's remaining reason to exist after
  the 146x finding, and it is wrong on this task.
- [SURPRISE] My pre-registered predictions scored 2/5. Right: gyro white no
  failure, latency most sensitive. Wrong: gyro bias "marginal, decides it"
  (no knee at all); gyro scale "possible" (50% error costs 3%); gravity tilt
  "no failure" (it is one of only two channels that do fail).
- NOTHING in the physically plausible BMI270 box breaks this policy. Closest
  margins are latency ~7x and gravity tilt ~10x; every gyro channel is
  25-635x clear.
- [SURPRISE] The policy was trained with noise_config.level = 0.0 -- NO
  observation DR at all -- and is still immune to 635x the BMI270 gyro noise.
  So the robustness does not come from DR hardening; it comes from the task
  having redundant state (joint encoders, gravity vector, command). This is
  stronger than the 146x framing: even an unhardened policy does not care.
- The two channels that DO bind -- gravity-vector error and latency -- are
  precisely the ones that live in the STATE ESTIMATOR, not in a datasheet
  noise spec. That is the raw-sensor-vs-estimator limitation showing up as a
  measurement rather than a worry.

- [SURPRISE] The gyro-scale fall-rate bump at magnitude 0.0224 (user spotted
  the non-monotonicity) is noise twice over. It is 2 episodes of 64, Wilson
  95% [0.009, 0.107] against [0, 0.057] at zero -- indistinguishable. AND it
  does not reproduce: across three runs with identical seeds that point read
  0.00, 0.031, 0.00.
- [LIMITATION] The sweep is NOT bit-reproducible on GPU. Floating-point
  reduction order is not fixed, and contact dynamics amplify ~1e-7
  differences over 1000 steps until marginal episodes flip. Measured
  run-to-run variation at near-zero fall rate: ~2/64 episodes, independent of
  sampling error. Bears directly on brief 9's reproducibility requirement; a
  single run's "zero" fall rate is not zero.
- [LIMITATION] My first figures had NO uncertainty at all, so a 2-episode
  fluctuation was drawn identically to a real knee. It was caught only
  because it happened to be non-monotonic -- a noise excursion pointing the
  convenient way would have read as a finding. Fixed: Wilson 95% bands on
  fall rate, +-1 SEM on return, and the sweep now saves per-point spread
  rather than means alone.
