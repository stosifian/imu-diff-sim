## Found

- [SURPRISE] My claim that "standard DR entirely omits structured errors" is
  FALSE as a statement about the field. Per-episode IMU bias randomization is
  documented in multiple quadruped sim-to-real works, including the RSS 2018
  Minitaur paper. It is accurate about MuJoCo Playground specifically, which
  is all that was measured. Surviving weaker claim: DR noise models are
  hand-tuned rather than physics-derived, and scale factor, temporal
  correlation, cross-axis coupling and temperature appear nowhere found.
- Turn-on bias magnitudes, re-derived independently: gyro bound (+-0.5 dps =
  8.73e-3 rad/s) is 184x the within-episode random-walk drift at T_ep = 20 s;
  accel is 603x.
- [SURPRISE] The findings' stated "60-190x" range brackets EPISODE LENGTH, not
  sensor: the gyro ratio runs 184x at 20 s down to 58x at 200 s. My 603x is
  the accelerometer at 20 s, a different quantity. No contradiction -- I
  compared the wrong things. Nothing to reconcile.
- THE NUMBER THAT MATTERS: at its datasheet bound, turn-on bias is 1.7x the
  Playground DR's episode-mean persistent component (8.73e-3 vs 5.16e-3 rad/s
  at a 500-step horizon). First parameter in the physical box that EXCEEDS
  what the policy was trained against. The 146x over-coverage holds for white
  noise and inverts for persistent bias.
- Mutation tests verified to fail for the right reason, not on a NaN: a
  sqrt(2) in the white-noise density moves N to 1.4132 and leaves K at 0.9812;
  a dropped /3 moves K to 1.7020 and leaves N at 0.9993; the clean case sits
  inside tolerance on both.

  while leaving the preinstalled jax_cuda13_plugin stale, so both plugins
  raced to register (cuda13 API mismatch, cuda12 ALREADY_EXISTS). It resolved
  to a GPU by luck. Colab ships a working CUDA JAX; do not reinstall it.
