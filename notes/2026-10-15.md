## Found

- Noise density recovers to 0.1% of input. Random walk recovers unbiased
  (ratio 1.036 gyro / 1.010 accel, z < 1.3) but with ~11% 1-sigma scatter PER
  REALIZATION. [LIMITATION] A single static log -- which is all a real device
  gives you -- cannot pin random walk better than about +-15%. Any comparison
  to the Luxonis value has to carry that error bar.
- [SURPRISE] A 5-realization average showed a 10.4% "bias" at ~4 sigma that
  vanished at 16 realizations. Nearly reported an estimator bug that was
  small-sample noise. The z-column in validate_model.py exists because of this.
- [LIMITATION] EuRoC sensor.yaml values are ESTIMATOR TUNING values, not an
  Allan characterization. ADIS16448 accel random walk 3.0e-3 m/s^3/sqrt(Hz) is
  ~40x the BMI270 figure and not plausible for that grade. The reference curve
  sits above our consumer part for that reason alone. Not usable as evidence.
- fit_allan.py --force on EuRoC V1_01 recovers gyro noise density 0.025
  rad/s/sqrt(Hz) against the published 1.70e-4 -- 150x high. Good demonstration
  that motion contamination is catastrophic, not subtle.

## Notes

- [DECISION] initial_bias returns zero, not a turn-on-bias draw. Turn-on bias
  would be nuisance randomness for the search unless held fixed, and it is
  degenerate with the thermal offset we do not model. [LIMITATION] This is the
  main place the model understates real-device error over a short rollout.
- [DECISION] No temperature term. Over a seconds-long rollout thermal drift is
  indistinguishable from a constant bias offset, so it would add a parameter
  the search cannot identify separately from initial_bias.
- [LIMITATION] Pure random walk means NO flicker floor. fit_bias_instability
  returns NaN on our synthetic traces by design. Real MEMS parts show a floor;
  this is a real gap between model and device.
- [DECISION] float64 enabled in tests and scripts. In float32 the
  finite-difference gradient test measures quantization noise (it returned
  exactly eps/2h), and the million-sample cumsum loses the long-tau end.
- [OPEN] SCALE_FACTOR_BOUND = 0.02 still has no datasheet citation. Tagged
  UNKNOWN in BMI270_PROVENANCE. Blocks the write-up, not the code.
- [OPEN] No real static log exists yet. External validation is still missing;
