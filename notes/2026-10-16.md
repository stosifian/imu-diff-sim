## Found

- [SURPRISE] Gradient CONDITIONING is fine. The feared chaotic-unroll failure
  did not appear: C1 finite at every H; C2 analytic-vs-central-difference
  agreement to 0.0% at every H; C4 ||g|| ~ H^0.51, sublinear. On this task the
  gradients are clean.
- [SURPRISE] The real blocker is task sensitivity, not gradient quality. Task
  loss moves 1.1% at H=100 and 0.1% at H=400 across the ENTIRE search box.
  Noise-scale probe: loss is flat to 1% until gyro noise density reaches 1000x
  nominal, and only reaches +47% at 1000x, +191% at 1e4x.
- Mechanism is clear and not subtle: nominal gyro noise std is 5.0e-4 rad/s
  against a true |omega| of 6.1e-2 rad/s -- a noise/signal ratio of 8e-3 --
  and only 3 of the ant's 27 observation entries are corrupted. The other 24
  are clean ground-truth state, so the policy simply routes around the IMU.
- C3 sign agreement: gyro_scale_factor 1.00, gyro_noise_density 0.62,
  gyro_random_walk 0.62. Consistent with the above -- the deterministic
  parameter has a stable gradient sign; the two stochastic ones do not,
  because their gradient is numerical noise around essentially zero signal.
- Three of six parameters are structurally inactive on the ant: the
  observation carries no linear acceleration, so no accelerometer parameter
  can influence the loss at all.
- Go1 env creation on CPU takes 1120 s (19 min), mostly mujoco_menagerie
  download and compile. [DECISION] Colab is now mandatory, not preferred.
- Go1 `state` observation is 48-dim: linvel 3, gyro 3, projected gravity 3,
  joint pos 12, joint vel 12, last action 12, command 3. [LIMITATION] It
  includes LOCAL LINEAR VELOCITY, which on real hardware comes from a state
  estimator, so the "no ground truth anywhere" framing is weaker than claimed
  when the task was chosen -- the policy retains a partial route around IMU
  corruption.
- [SURPRISE] THE MEASUREMENT THAT REFRAMED THE PROJECT. MuJoCo Playground
  applies uniform gyro DR of +-0.2 rad/s (std 0.1155). A BMI270 at the 50 Hz
  control rate has gyro white-noise std 7.9e-4 rad/s. Ratio 146x. Gravity DR
  is +-0.05 against ~0.02 tilt from a 2% accel scale error, so 2.5x.
- The same `gyro=0.2, gravity=0.05` appears across SEVEN Playground robots --
  Go1 joystick/handstand/getup, G1, Berkeley Humanoid, Apollo, T1 --
  quadrupeds and bipeds alike. Not per-robot calibration against a sensor
  spec; a copied default.

## Notes

- [DECISION] Brax ant is unusable for this project's claim, and the reason is
  the observation space, not the physics backend. Any task whose policy sees
  clean ground-truth state cannot exhibit IMU-induced failure modes inside a
  physically plausible noise box. Switching to a Go1/Barkour-style joystick
  task -- where the observation is gyro + projected gravity + joint angles +
  command, with no ground-truth state -- is now required, not preferred.
- [SURPRISE] This inverts the plan's risk assessment. Weeks of planning treated
  chaotic contact gradients as the main threat; the measured threat is that
  the task is insensitive to the parameters being searched.
- [LIMITATION] Policy is a randomly-initialized MLP, not trained. A trained
  gait may be more fragile. But a 1000x-nominal insensitivity margin will not
  be closed by policy quality alone.
- [OPEN] Does a Go1-style observation actually restore sensitivity? Must be
  measured with the same probe before committing week 2. If even a
  blind-to-state policy needs >5x nominal noise to fail, the claim needs
  reframing.
- [DECISION] The original claim is not testable against a stock Go1 policy. A
  policy trained with gyro DR 146x the real sensor cannot fail inside the
  physical box, for gradient search or uniform sampling alike -- the week-3
  comparison would have been vacuous. Learned before building it.
- [OPEN] Four directions considered: retrain with realistic noise then search;
  keep the stock policy and search only bias/scale; ship the negative result;
  widen the box beyond BMI270. Not resolved -- superseded next day by the
  decision to measure sensitivity first.
- [LIMITATION] The 146x compares DR against BENCH noise. A gyro on a trotting
  quadruped sees structural vibration and impact transients that no Allan
  curve captures. If installed noise is ~0.1 rad/s the DR level is well
  calibrated and this framing compares the wrong two quantities.
- Bug found and fixed by the spike: params._log_bounds used float(jnp.log(..)),
  which raises ConcretizationTypeError under jit. Every jitted call site --
  i.e. the whole week-3 search -- would have hit it. Regression test added.
