## Found

- Two of the three "sanity envelope" citations in the brief are mis-cited.
  arXiv 2005.08284 is a wheeled-robot IMU calibration paper; arXiv 2603.26740
  is a monocular VIO scale-observability paper. Neither is an Allan
  characterization. Suvorkin 2024 (10.3390/s24061953) is real and is a better
  fit than the brief suggests: three MEMS grades, week-long static, overlapping
  ADEV. [LIMITATION] The consumer-MEMS envelope now rests on one paper plus
  our own log, not three sources.
- Luxonis depthai-core#886 verified digit-for-digit against the brief. Adds
  context the brief omits: 10 h at 100 Hz, and the poster's own doubt about
  whether the OAK stream is raw. [LIMITATION] gyroscope_random_walk = 1.06e-5
  is the weakest number in params.py -- one device, in a camera, possible CRT
  artifact.
- EuRoC V1 sequences cannot validate the Allan estimator: no static segment
  anywhere (quietest 10 s window peaks at 0.58 rad/s) and only 85-145 s long.
